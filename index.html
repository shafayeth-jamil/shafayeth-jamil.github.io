Research

Papers

2026

Charge conservation fixes the expansion of Bohmian congruences

Under reviewCanadian Journal of Physics, submitted September 2026

A quantum Raychaudhuri equation built from de Broglie–Bohm trajectories has been used to argue that quantum effects prevent geodesic focusing and remove the big-bang singularity. I show that the normalized Bohmian flow is the unit flow of the conserved Klein–Gordon current, so its expansion is fixed by charge conservation and the equation adds no dynamics beyond the continuity equation. For a minimally coupled scalar the flow has zero vorticity, and the quantum potential cannot reverse null focusing for any state; it survives in the null channel only through a positive effective-mass factor. In flat FRW cosmology, charge conservation sets a dust-like floor on the energy density, so there is no bounce and the scale factor reaches zero in finite proper time.

The cosmological result is exact under FRW symmetry and minimal coupling only.

2026

Hyperbolicity and the initial value formulation of an Einstein–entropy–clock model

In preparation

Gravity coupled to an entropy scalar and a clock field whose normalized gradient defines a hypersurface-orthogonal preferred frame, with the normalization imposed by a Lagrange multiplier and a derivative coupling between the entropy and the expansion of the preferred flow. In spherical symmetry I write the system in first-order quasilinear form with an eleven-component state vector and compute its principal symbol. The constraints propagate through the contracted Bianchi identity and Noether's second theorem. Strong hyperbolicity holds only on a codimension-one locus in state space; off that locus the characteristic matrix carries a Jordan block, so local well-posedness is conditional.

2025

Galactic dynamics without dark matter: a phenomenological comparison of MOND and emergent gravity

PreprintPreprints.org, December 2025

A comparison of MOND and Verlinde's emergent gravity as alternatives to particle dark matter at galactic scales, through flat rotation curves, the baryonic Tully–Fisher relation and the radial acceleration relation. MOND gives more tightly constrained predictions; the emergent-gravity predictions rest on assumptions of symmetry, isolation and equilibrium. Written up from my MS thesis.

MS thesis

2024–2025
Galactic Dynamics and Cosmological Observables without Particle Dark Matter: A Comparative Study of MOND (AQUAL/QUMOND/TeVeS) and Emergent Gravity Supervisor: Prof. Anjan Kumar Chowdhury, JNICAR, University of Chittagong

Covers the thermodynamic route to gravitational dynamics, from the Unruh effect and local Rindler thermodynamics to Jacobson's derivation of the Einstein equations from the Clausius relation, then Verlinde's entropic and de Sitter emergent gravity. The comparison with the MOND family points to the lack of a covariant action principle as the main structural gap in the emergent framework.
