Charge conservation fixes the expansion of Bohmian congruences

The quantum Raychaudhuri equation on Bohmian trajectories adds nothing beyond charge conservation, and the quantum potential cannot reverse null focusing for any state.

This equation has been used to argue that quantum effects prevent focusing and remove the big-bang singularity. I show that the normalized Bohmian flow is the unit flow of the conserved Klein–Gordon current, so its expansion is fixed by charge conservation. For a minimally coupled scalar the flow has zero vorticity, and the quantum potential survives in the null channel only through a positive effective-mass factor. In flat FRW cosmology, charge conservation sets a dust-like floor on the energy density, so there is no bounce and the scale factor reaches zero in finite proper time.

The cosmological result is exact under FRW symmetry and minimal coupling only.

Hyperbolicity and the initial value formulation of an Einstein–entropy–clock model

The constraints propagate, but strong hyperbolicity holds only on a codimension-one locus in state space, so local well-posedness is conditional.

Gravity coupled to an entropy scalar and a clock field whose normalized gradient defines a hypersurface-orthogonal preferred frame, with the normalization imposed by a Lagrange multiplier and a derivative coupling between the entropy and the expansion of the preferred flow. In spherical symmetry I write the system in first-order quasilinear form with an eleven-component state vector and compute its principal symbol. The constraints propagate through the contracted Bianchi identity and Noether's second theorem. Off the semisimplicity locus the characteristic matrix carries a Jordan block.

Galactic dynamics without dark matter: a phenomenological comparison of MOND and emergent gravity

MOND gives the more tightly constrained description of galactic scaling relations; the emergent-gravity predictions rest on assumptions of symmetry, isolation and equilibrium.

A comparison of MOND and Verlinde's emergent gravity as alternatives to particle dark matter at galactic scales, through flat rotation curves, the baryonic Tully–Fisher relation and the radial acceleration relation. Written up from my MS thesis.
